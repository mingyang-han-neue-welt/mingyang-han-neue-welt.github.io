Research Overview

This research program develops the "Motion-Staticity" (MS) reconstruction framework. It begins with a bounded vector-scalar state for a massive process and asks how far relativistic structure can be recovered by requiring compatible operational and canonical representations. The resulting relativistic massive-generator geometry is then used as input for an operational reconstruction of Lorentzian soldering and spacetime geometry, followed by two distinct downstream directions: a restricted classical gravitational and asymptotic-boundary branch, and a local quantum soldering-kinematics branch.

The primitive massive MS state is described by a motion component $\mathbf{D}$ and a scalar staticity component $S$ satisfying

$$ |\mathbf{D}|^2 + S^2 = 1, \quad S > 0 $$

This quadratic relation is an internal Euclidean state-space normalization; it is not introduced as a spacetime metric. In the operational radial realization, $\mathbf{D}$ becomes $\mathbf{v}/c$, while the scalar component becomes $S=d\tau/dt$ after the additive intrinsic scalar accumulation is identified with proper time through the induced Lorentzian chronometry.

Requiring the same MS state to admit a compatible canonical representation with an initially unspecified free Hamiltonian $H(\mathbf{p})$ gives the consistency equation

$$ \frac{|\nabla_{\mathbf p}H|^2}{c^2} + \frac{\left(H-\mathbf p\cdot\nabla_{\mathbf p}H\right)^2}{c^2Q^2} =1, \qquad H-\mathbf p\cdot\nabla_{\mathbf p}H>0. $$

For a finite convex $C^1$ Hamiltonian with the calibrated rest value $H(\mathbf{0})=cQ$, satisfying this relation globally and giving a complete canonical representation of the massive MS state manifold, the free Hamiltonian is uniquely

$$ H(\mathbf{p}) = c\sqrt{|\mathbf{p}|^2 + Q^2}. $$

The corresponding positive massive-generator geometry supplies the Lorentzian internal structure used in the curved-spacetime reconstruction. A scalar generator-displacement response then reconstructs a nondegenerate solder map $e:TM\to V$ and the metric $g=e^*\eta$. From this point the program separates into distinct developments: under additional restricted canonical assumptions one obtains an ADM/Einstein classical branch and, with asymptotic boundary conditions, a total translation charge; alternatively, treating the solder map as a configuration variable and adding coherent soldering deformations produces a local Weyl-type quantum kinematics.

Paper II: Operational Soldering from Relativistic Massive-State Geometry: An Inverse Reconstruction
The Author
Abstract: Taking the relativistic massive-generator geometry obtained in the flat-space analysis as input, we formulate an inverse reconstruction of local Lorentzian spacetime geometry. An internal rank-four Lorentz bundle $V\to M$ is initially independent of the tangent bundle $TM$, with no spacetime metric or solder form assumed. A scalar generator-displacement response $\Theta_x(\Pi,\xi)$, defined on the positive massive-generator cone and tangent displacements, is required to satisfy composition, positive homogeneity, continuity, smooth base-point dependence, and operational faithfulness. These conditions give a unique bilinear extension and reconstruct a unique nondegenerate solder map $$ e_x:T_xM\longrightarrow V_x, \qquad g=e^*\eta. $$ Absolute response calibration fixes the spacetime metric scale, whereas response data known only up to local positive rescaling determine only the conformal class. As a separate and conditional downstream result, imposing a torsion-free branch, metric ADM phase space, the standard Lorentzian hypersurface-deformation algebra, and a restricted quadratic scalar constraint recovers the ADM/Einstein branch by off-shell closure.

Paper III: From Operational Soldering to Quantum Configuration Space: Coherent Deformations and Projective Comparison
The Author
Abstract: Starting from the operationally reconstructed Lorentzian solder map, we study the local quantum kinematics associated with allowing the soldering configuration to vary. At each event the ambient configuration space is $\mathcal{E}_x=\mathrm{Hom}(T_xM,V_x)$, and the response functions $\Theta_{\Pi,\xi}(e)=\Pi(e\xi)$ span its linear dual and separate configurations. An exact unitary implementation that preserves additive composition independently in the generator and displacement arguments is necessarily Abelian, so the classical response alone does not generate noncommutative kinematics. Introducing the additional principle of coherent soldering translations $e\mapsto e+A$ and the standard cotangent completion $T^*\mathcal{E}_x$ yields $$ [\hat{K}(A), \hat{\Theta}_{\Pi,\xi}] = i\hbar\,\Pi(A\xi)I, $$ while the response observables themselves remain mutually commuting. On the nondegenerate sector, zero-level internal Lorentz reduction recovers the cotangent bundle of oriented, time-oriented Lorentz metric configuration space. A separate projective comparison construction provides a conditional diagnostic of spin-cover parity. The construction is local and kinematical; it is not a quantization of general relativity or a derivation of matter.

Paper IV: From Local Response to Global Momentum: Asymptotic Boundary Completion in Motion-Staticity Geometry
The Author
Abstract: Returning to the restricted classical branch, we study how a locally reconstructed gravitational geometry acquires a global translation generator for an isolated system. On calibrated asymptotically Minkowskian ends, standard Regge-Teitelboim falloff and parity conditions are imposed together with the response calibration required at the translation-sensitive asymptotic orders. Allowing the reconstructed bulk generators to approach constant translations produces a surface obstruction to functional differentiability. Its boundary primitive defines a total translation covector $\mathsf{P}^{\mathrm{MS}}_\infty\in\mathfrak{t}^*_\infty$. The resulting observer-energy function reconstructs the complete total momentum and exhibits a local-global response reciprocity between proper-time norm and invariant mass. After imposing the empirical Einstein normalization $\kappa=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}$, the completed MS translation charge agrees with the ADM four-momentum: $$ E_\infty^{\mathrm{MS}} = E_{\mathrm{ADM}}, \quad \mathbf{P}_\infty^{\mathrm{MS}} = \mathbf{P}_{\mathrm{ADM}}. $$ The construction does not derive the value of $G$ or a new positive-mass theorem; its contribution is the operational provenance and structural placement of the asymptotic translation charge within the MS framework.
