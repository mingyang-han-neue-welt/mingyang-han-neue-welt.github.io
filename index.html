Research Overview

This research program investigates the "Motion-Staticity" (MS) geometric framework. Starting from a compact vector-scalar state, we explore the rigidity of relativistic free-particle Hamiltonians, reconstruct Lorentzian soldering maps and spacetime metrics via operational generator-displacement responses, and discuss the quantum configuration space and global boundary completion.

The core of this framework lies in the metric tensor $g_{\mu\nu}$ on a spacetime manifold and its boundary dynamics. Locally, the system satisfies a quadratic relation determined by the compact state:

$$ |\mathbf{D}|^2 + S^2 = 1, \quad S > 0 $$

where $\mathbf{D}$ represents the motion degrees of freedom and $S$ represents the staticity. By requiring the same state to admit a canonical representation, one uniquely derives the relativistic free Hamiltonian $H(\mathbf{p}) = c\sqrt{|\mathbf{p}|^2 + Q^2}$, extending the local geometry to quantum configuration spaces and the global momentum of asymptotically flat spacetimes.
